Community Guidelines

Last updated: November 2026

8mellow is a place to say what you never got to say, gently. These guidelines keep it safe for everyone who writes here and everyone who reads.

The short version

💜 Be kind

Speak about your own feelings. Do not use this space to hurt, shame or attack anyone.

🔒 Protect identities

No real names, contact details or anything that could identify you or another person.

🚫 No harassment

No bullying, threats, intimidation or repeated unwanted contact.

🙅 Respect "no"

If someone does not want to talk, stop. If they block you, do not try to reach them another way.

🔞 Adults only

You must be 18 or older. No sexual content, and nothing involving minors.

1. Be kind

Many messages here are apologies, thank-yous, goodbyes and hard truths. Write from your own experience and your own feelings. You can say how something affected you without insulting, mocking or blaming a person in a way meant to hurt them. When you read or reply, assume the other person is carrying something heavy.

2. Do not identify people (no doxxing)

Public messages are anonymous, and that protection only works if everyone keeps it that way. Do not share:

  • Real names, nicknames or usernames from other platforms, of yourself or anyone else.
  • Phone numbers, email addresses, home or work addresses, or social media handles.
  • Details specific enough to point to one person, such as a full workplace, class or street.
  • Photos, screenshots or documents showing another person.

Do not try to work out who wrote a message, and do not share guesses about who it might be. Keep it about the feelings, not the identity.

3. No harassment, threats or hate

  • No threats of violence or harm, including "jokes" about hurting someone.
  • No bullying, name-calling, humiliation or pressure on someone to respond.
  • No hateful content targeting people for their race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, age or any similar characteristic.
  • No encouraging anyone to harm themselves.

4. Private chats

A private conversation begins only when someone chooses to reply. Replying is an invitation, not a promise of a relationship.

  • It is always okay not to reply, to end a conversation, or to delete it from your inbox.
  • Do not pressure, guilt or repeatedly message someone who has gone quiet.
  • Do not ask for personal details, money, photos or to move the conversation to another app.
  • Do not use a private chat to threaten, blackmail or expose someone.
  • If you are blocked, that is final. Do not make a new account to contact the same person.

5. Adults only, and no sexual content

8mellow is for people 18 and over. Sexual or explicit content is not allowed anywhere on the site. Anything that sexualizes or endangers a minor is removed immediately and may be reported to the authorities.

6. No spam, scams or impersonation

  • No advertising, promotion, links to sell things, or requests for money.
  • No pretending to be another person, an 8mellow team member, or someone you are not.
  • No fake accounts, and no using more than one account to get around a block or a removal.

7. If you or someone else is in danger

8mellow is not a crisis service. If you are thinking about harming yourself, or you are worried about someone's safety, please contact your local emergency number or a crisis line. You can find options on our Safety & Support page.

8. Reporting and blocking

If something breaks these guidelines, use Report this chat or Block this user in the Safety menu of any private conversation. Reports are reviewed by our safety team. You do not have to explain a block, and the other person is not told when you block them.

9. What happens when the rules are broken

Depending on how serious the problem is and whether it has happened before, we may:

  • Hide or remove a message or conversation.
  • Send a warning.
  • Temporarily limit an account.
  • Permanently close an account.
  • Report serious threats, or anything involving minors, to the appropriate authorities.

We may act without warning for serious violations. If you believe we made a mistake, email us at support@8mellow.com and we will take a second look.

10. Updates

As the community grows, these guidelines may change. We will update the date at the top of this page when they do.
