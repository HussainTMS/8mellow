How It Works

A safe, anonymous way to connect with people from your past.

📝

1. Post a Message

Share what's on your mind for someone from your past. Choose a category like apology, goodbye, or thank you.

🔍

2. Find the Right Person

Your message is posted anonymously on the public wall. Anyone can read it, but no one knows it's from you.

🛡️

3. Connect Safely

If someone wants to reply, they must create an account. This keeps the community safe and prevents spam.

🔒

4. Chat Privately

Once they reply, a private conversation opens. Only you two can see it. No one else can join or read it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my message really anonymous?
Yes. When you post on the public wall, your name and email are never shown. You can also choose to leave your Display Name blank, making you fully anonymous. Only you know which messages are yours.
Can anyone message me directly?
No. We have a strict "No Cold DMs" policy. The only way someone can start a private chat with you is by replying to one of your public messages. You will never receive unsolicited private messages.
What happens if someone reports my message?
If a message is reported, it is automatically hidden from the public wall while our safety team reviews it. If it violates our guidelines (e.g., contains real names, threats, or harassment), it will be permanently removed.
Can I delete a message I posted?
Yes. Go to "My Messages" from your dashboard. You can hide a message from the wall or delete it permanently at any time.
Is 8mellow a crisis service?
No. 8mellow is not a therapy service, a crisis hotline, or a substitute for professional mental health care. If you are in crisis, please contact a local emergency service or a crisis helpline immediately.
