Help & FAQs

Answers to common questions about how 8mellow works.

How it works

Getting started

What is 8mellow?

8mellow is a place to share the things you never got to say, anonymously. You can read other people's messages, show support, and start a private conversation if a message speaks to you.

Do I need an account?

You can read the wall without one. To write a message, press Me too, reply privately or search, you need to sign up.

How do I sign in?

There is no password. Enter your email address and we send you a secure, one-time sign-in link. Open it and you are signed in. The link works once and expires after a short time, so request a new one if it stops working.

Who can use 8mellow?

You must be 18 or older. Please read our Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

Privacy and anonymity

Is my message really anonymous?

Your name and email address are never shown. If you add a User ID, school, current location or bio to your profile, those details appear next to your messages and logged-in members can search them. Leave them empty to stay fully anonymous.

People who know you may still recognise your story, so avoid details that point to you or to someone else.

Who can see my private chats?

Only you and the other person. We do not read private chats unless one of you reports it, or we are legally required to. See our Privacy Policy.

Can I delete my message?

Yes. In My Messages you can hide a message from the wall or delete it. Deleting a message does not delete private chats that started from it. Those stay in each person's inbox until they delete them.

How do I delete my account?

Go to My Profile from the Account menu on your dashboard and choose delete account.

Writing a message

Why was my message not posted?

To protect everyone's anonymity, messages cannot contain email addresses, phone numbers, links or @handles. Hateful language is also not allowed. Remove those parts and try again. Strong feelings and strong words are fine, but threats and harassment are not allowed.

Can I save a message and finish it later?

Yes. Your unfinished message is saved on the device you are using, for up to 7 days, and your dashboard offers to continue it. It is not saved on other devices, and it is removed when you post or sign out.

What is the search bar on the wall?

Logged-in members can search messages and the details shown next to them, such as a school, a current location or words in a bio, to find people with shared experiences or interests.

Messages and chats

What does "Me too" mean?

It is a quiet way to tell the writer you have felt something similar. They see the count on their message, but not who pressed it.

How do private replies work?

Press Reply privately on a message to start a one-to-one chat with its writer. Your conversations are in your Inbox, and a number on the Inbox card shows how many are unread.

What if someone makes me uncomfortable?

Open the chat and use the Safety menu: Block stops them contacting you and they are not told, and Report sends the chat to our safety team. You never have to reply to anyone.

If I delete a chat, is it deleted for the other person too?

No. Deleting a conversation removes it from your inbox only, and the other person keeps their copy. If they send you a new message, the chat comes back to your inbox. Messages may be kept for a short time so our safety team can review any report.

Your account

Why was I signed out?

For your privacy, we sign you out after 30 minutes of inactivity. You see a warning first, and you can choose to stay signed in.

How do I change my name or email?

Open Settings from the Account menu on your dashboard. A change of email address is confirmed by a link sent to the new address.

Support and safety

Is 8mellow a crisis or counselling service?

No. 8mellow is not a replacement for professional help. If you are struggling or in danger, please contact your local emergency number or a crisis line. You can find options on our Safety & Support page, including 9-8-8 in Canada.

I have another question or a problem

Email us at support@8mellow.com and we will get back to you.
