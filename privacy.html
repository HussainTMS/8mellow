Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2025

1. Who We Are

8mellow is a platform for sharing anonymous messages with people from your past, and for having private, two-way conversations when someone replies. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the rights you have.

2. The Short Version

  • We collect as little as possible. No real names, no addresses, no phone numbers.
  • Your public messages are anonymous. No one sees who wrote them.
  • Your private chats are private. Only you and the other person can see them.
  • We do not sell your data. Ever.
  • You can delete your account and your data at any time.

3. What We Collect

When you create an account:

  • Email address (for login and password reset).
  • Password (stored securely as a hash).
  • Display Name (optional). If you leave it blank, you remain fully anonymous.

When you post a public message:

  • The text of your message.
  • The category and time you posted it.
  • A record that you are the author (visible only to you).

When you use private chats:

  • The messages you and the other person send.
  • A record of who is in the conversation (visible only to the two of you).

4. What We Do NOT Collect

  • Your real name.
  • Your home address.
  • Your phone number.
  • Your location.
  • Your contacts.

5. How We Use Your Information

We use your information only to let you log in securely, show your messages on the public wall (anonymously), deliver private chats, and detect abuse or harmful content. We do not use your information for advertising or sell it to third parties.

6. Who Can See What

  • Your email and password: Only you and our secure auth system.
  • Your public message: Everyone (anonymously).
  • Your private chat: Only you and the other person.
  • Your report history: Only 8mellow's safety team.

7. Moderation & Safety

To keep 8mellow safe, we run every new public message through an automated filter before it appears on the wall. Flagged messages are reviewed by our safety team. We do not read your private chats unless a report has been filed and we are investigating it, or we are legally required to do so.

8. Data Retention

Public messages are kept until you delete them or delete your account. Private chats are deleted when either person deletes their account. If you delete your account, your email and login data are removed within 30 days, public messages are anonymized, and private chats are deleted.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your data at any time. To exercise any of these rights, email us at [support@8mellow.com].

10. Children

8mellow is for adults only. You must be 18 or older to create an account. If we discover that a user is under 18, we will remove the account and its data.

11. Security

We use industry-standard security: encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, two-factor authentication, and row-level access controls in our database. However, no system is 100% secure. If we ever experience a data breach that affects you, we will notify you within 72 hours.

12. Not a Crisis Service

8mellow is not a therapy service, not a crisis hotline, and not a substitute for professional mental health care. If you are in crisis, please contact a local emergency service or a crisis helpline.

13. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, email us at [support@8mellow.com].
