You are not alone

If you are in crisis or need someone to talk to, support is available. These helplines are free, confidential and open 24/7.

Get immediate help

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call your local emergency number (911 in Canada and the US, 999 in the UK).

🇨🇦 Canada: 9-8-8 Suicide Crisis Helpline (call or text) 🇺🇸 US: 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline 🇬🇧 UK: Samaritans (116 123) 🌍 Anywhere else: Find a helpline

Staying safe on 8mellow

🚩
Report. In any private chat, open the Safety menu and choose "Report this chat". Our safety team will review it.
🚫
Block. In the same menu, "Block this user" stops them contacting you. They are not told.
🗑️
Delete. You can remove any conversation from your inbox at any time with the bin icon.
🔒
Stay anonymous. Never share real names, contact details or where you live, even if someone asks.
